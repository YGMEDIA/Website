Learning · Web app · Free to start

Paukbox. Flashcards that stick.

Learn vocabulary and technical terms as flashcards, right in the browser on phone, tablet and desktop. Lists come in via PDF import, as plain text or straight from an AI in the format "word = meaning". The Leitner system repeats at intervals of 1, 3, 7 and 21 days, and quiz rounds in between lock the knowledge in. 50 cards are free, unlimited cards cost a one-time 2 euros.

Partner · Investor · Reference case

Felgen Brillant. The real business our products are built on.

Felgen Brillant refurbishes damaged original wheel rims entirely in-house: straightening, CNC diamond cutting, powder coating, painting, plus its own shop for refurbished brand rims. YG MEDIA builds the digital infrastructure around the company, from marketing and the website to its own software.

Felgen Brillant finances the development of USELY as an investor. Every module proves itself in daily operations first, with real orders, receipts and marketplace sales, before it is rolled out to all users.

Company

YG MEDIA®,

YG MEDIA is the digital studio of Yasin Gündogdu in Würzburg, Germany, working in software, marketing and robotics. More than ten years at the intersection of design, technology and marketing: over 60 companies from Germany, Austria and Switzerland built up digitally, from trades and medical practices to industry and tech start-ups.
